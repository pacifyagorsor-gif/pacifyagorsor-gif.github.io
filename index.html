01 / ABOUT

Researching the forces that shape the built environment.

I work at the intersection of real estate, geospatial science, and finance. My interests include property market behaviour, digital transformation in real estate, climate risk, and the use of spatial evidence to better understand places and property outcomes.

My academic background spans real estate, geospatial science, and finance. I am interested in research that connects rigorous analysis with practical questions facing property markets and the built environment in Ghana and beyond.
